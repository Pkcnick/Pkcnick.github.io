Research

Main Research Themes

Theme 1

Graph-Based Bayesian Optimization for Quantum Architecture Search

Development of graph neural network surrogates and uncertainty-aware acquisition strategies for optimizing variational quantum circuit architectures under hardware- and noise-aware constraints.

Theme 2

Hybrid Quantum Neural Networks for Time-Series Prediction

Hybrid quantum-classical models for financial time-series regression using custom variational quantum circuits and classical neural components.

Theme 3

Quantum Machine Learning for Quantum Error Correction

Learning-based decoders, transfer-aware evaluation, and quantum architecture search strategies for error correction codes such as Five-Qubit and Steane codes.
